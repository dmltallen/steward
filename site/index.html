Steward turns the computer you already own into an always-on personal assistant. It detects your machine, picks the right open models, and serves them to your phone and desktop. No subscription, no account, no data leaving your network.

How it works Free to install

Free to install and use. A perpetual license keeps working offline forever, with nothing to renew.

Runs on what you own: any machine with 16 GB or more of usable memory, computed at install, not certified. 40 GB unlocks the full experience.

Privacy by design: chats, files and memory stay on your box. The only outbound calls are the model download and update checks.

Zero setup decisions

It detects memory, GPU class, and bandwidth, then auto-selects the model bundle that fits. The hard choice is made for you.

Reachable everywhere

One always-on assistant on the LAN, reachable from your phone and your computer. No cloud hop, no account, no data leaving home.

Yours to keep

Perpetual license, offline forever. Open models, MIT source. Capability it gives you once keeps working without renewals.

Compatibility is computed, not certified

Steward probes your machine at install and returns a verdict: full, lite, or no-fit. The floor is 16 GB of usable memory; 40 GB unlocks image understanding. No hardware is certified; your machine is whatever the check says it is that day.

Platforms are stated the same way. Binaries exist for Linux arm64 today, and the installation docs say so; Windows, macOS and amd64 are not built yet.

No account, no telemetry

There is no cloud account and no telemetry. Everything stored lives in one owner-only folder on your machine, and deleting that folder deletes it all.

Privacy

The promise, stated concretely.

  • One folder. Conversations, facts, the index built from your folders, settings and logs live under ~/.steward by default, owner-only. Move it with STEWARD_HOME.
  • No telemetry. The web client makes zero external requests; the phone app talks only to the host you pair it with, over your LAN.
  • Two outbound calls total. Downloading a model the first time, and a signed update check. Nothing else ever leaves the machine.
  • Delete it all. rm -rf ~/.steward. The full statement is in docs/PRIVACY.md.

Why Steward
